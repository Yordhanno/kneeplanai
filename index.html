01 · TECHNOLOGY

From radiograph to structured alignment data.

KneePlanAI is being developed to identify anatomical landmarks and calculate clinically relevant coronal alignment parameters from full-length standing radiographs.

01
↥

Input radiograph

Full-length AP lower-limb radiography provides the anatomical reference for the analysis.

02

The model identifies key hip, knee and ankle landmarks required for angular measurements.

Alignment parameters are generated automatically from the detected anatomical references.

Derived measurements support standardized interpretation, including CPAK phenotype analysis.

02 · MEASUREMENTS

One workflow. Multiple alignment parameters.

HKA

Hip–Knee–Ankle

Global coronal alignment of the lower limb.

mLDFA

Mechanical LDFA

Distal femoral coronal orientation.

MPTA

Medial Proximal Tibial Angle

Proximal tibial coronal orientation.

JLCA

Joint Line Convergence Angle

Relationship between distal femoral and proximal tibial joint lines.

aLDFA

Anatomic LDFA

Anatomical femoral axis relative to the distal joint line.

AMA

Anatomic–Mechanical Angle

Relationship between anatomical and mechanical femoral axes.

aHKA

Arithmetic HKA

Estimate of constitutional limb alignment from femoral and tibial geometry.

JLO

Joint Line Obliquity

Orientation of the joint line used in CPAK classification.

04 · ABOUT

KneePlanAI

KneePlanAI is an independent research and development initiative focused on applying artificial intelligence to radiographic alignment analysis in knee arthroplasty.

Current status: research prototype. Not intended to replace clinical judgment or serve as a standalone medical diagnosis or treatment tool.
