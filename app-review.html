Quick review steps

  1. Launch KneePlanAI Standard.
  2. Select Open radiograph and open the downloaded DICOM file.
  3. Select the right side.
  4. Place or adjust the anatomical landmarks and joint lines.
  5. Open Results, Report, and Analysis.
  6. Select Sources in the main toolbar to view the scientific methodology and linked citations.
